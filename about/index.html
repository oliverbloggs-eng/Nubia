#How it works

Simple, Whichever Side You’re On

  1. 1

    Property Onboarding

    Week 1

    We inspect the property, review documents and agree the management terms with you.

  2. 2

    Tenant Takeover or Placement

    Weeks 1–3

    We take over existing tenants or find and vet new ones.

  3. 3

    System Setup

    Week 2

    Rent collection via M-Pesa and bank, plus your online reporting, are set up.

  4. 4

    Active Management

    Ongoing

    Rent collection, tenant issues and repairs handled day to day.

  5. 5

    Monthly Reporting

    Every month

    A clear statement of income, expenses and property condition.
